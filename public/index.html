Understand AQI categories and pollutants

0–50GoodSafe outdoor activities
51–100ModerateSensitive caution
101–200PoorReduce outdoor exposure
201–300UnhealthyAvoid prolonged stay outside
301–400SevereStay indoors

🌫️ PM2.5

Moderate Risk

Sources: Traffic, smoke, burning

Effect: Enters lungs deeply

Tip: Wear mask & avoid dust

🏗️ PM10

Moderate Risk

Sources: Road dust, construction

Effect: Irritates nose/throat

Tip: Avoid dusty zones

🚗 CO

High Risk

Sources: Exhaust, generators

Effect: Reduces oxygen in blood

Tip: Stay ventilated

🚙 NO₂

Moderate Risk

Sources: Vehicles, fuel burning

Effect: Lung irritation

Tip: Avoid traffic areas

🏭 SO₂

High Risk

Sources: Factories, coal burning

Effect: Causes breathing issues

Tip: Stay indoors near smoke

🧴 VOC

Moderate Risk

Sources: Paints, perfumes, chemicals

Effect: Headache / irritation

Tip: Keep room ventilated

🧪 NH₃

Moderate Risk

Sources: Waste, fertilizer

Effect: Eye/nose irritation

Tip: Avoid strong odor areas
